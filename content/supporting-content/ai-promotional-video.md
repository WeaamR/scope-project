# SCOPE — AI-Generated Promotional Video

- **Tool:** Google Vids
- **Scenes:** 10 scenes, produced and assembled into one final video
- **Format:** 16:9 landscape
- **Style:** Photorealistic cinematic commercial
- **Narration:** Off-screen female Arabic voiceover, Palestinian/Levantine delivery
- **Character continuity:** The final assembled video was confirmed by the user to preserve the same character across the ten scenes.

## Story Sequence
1. A freelancer receives a new client notification.
2. She opens the message and begins reading.
3. She realizes the request is unclear and considers what to ask.
4. The camera reveals the SCOPE platform on her laptop.
5. She enters the client's request and starts analysis.
6. She reviews the AI analysis and suggested questions.
7. She reviews and uses the suggested professional client message.
8. After the client's reply, she prepares a scope draft for review.
9. She reviews agreement versions and project change history.
10. The commercial ends with the SCOPE brand and slogan added during editing.

## Prompt Engineering Techniques Documented
- Character identity lock and environment lock.
- Exact-frame continuation between consecutive scenes.
- Camera movement and continuity instructions.
- Native audio and off-screen voiceover instructions.
- Exact Arabic voiceover scripts and pronunciation constraints.
- Product UI constraints to avoid invented charts, statistics, and unsupported features.
- Negative instructions for no cuts, no subtitles, no extra characters, and no generated logo/text where these were not wanted.

## Prompt Improvement Example
The first final-scene prompt allowed a transition to a new dark navy brand frame. The generated result changed the woman's appearance. The revised prompt added strict character identity and environment locks, maintained the original woman and workspace through the final frame, prohibited character regeneration and camera-axis changes, and reserved logo/slogan placement for post-production. The user confirmed the assembled final video maintained character consistency.
