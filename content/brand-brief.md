# SCOPE — Mini Brand Brief

## Project

SCOPE is an AI-powered Freelance Scope Management Assistant.

## Initial Target Audience

Beginner freelancers, initially with a focus on graphic designers.
Future versions may expand to freelancers in additional disciplines.

## Content Goal

1.  Explain the problem of scope creep and unclear client requests.
2.  Introduce SCOPE and its real capabilities.
3.  Encourage freelancers to register and try the platform.

## Tone of Voice

Approachable, clear, conversational Arabic. The supplied carousel uses
colloquial Arabic to make the freelancer’s pain points relatable.

## Visual Direction

The supplied four-slide carousel uses a consistent white, black, and
yellow palette, grid/paper-like backgrounds, bold typography, and a
recurring black-cat character as a visual storytelling device. The
images in `main-content/carousel/` are the supplied final carousel
assets; they are not recreated in this package.

## Main Content

Four-slide social media carousel. See `main-content/carousel/`.

## Supporting Content

Ten-scene AI-generated promotional video produced with Google Vids. See
`supporting-content/ai-promotional-video.md`.
